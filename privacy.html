Moon Farm (the "Game") is made by Tember Studio (Taksapad Moonpim) ("we"). This policy explains what data we collect, how, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and how you can have it deleted.

The Game has no ads, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell data. It does not access your location, contacts or photo library (except the one picture you choose to upload as your profile picture).

2. Service providers who receive data

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: sign-in, cloud save, game servers) — all data in section 1 except email
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Rekognition) — checks profile pictures for inappropriate content before they are shown; we do not have AWS keep the picture
  • Supabase — stores approved profile pictures; they are reachable by a public link so other players can see them
  • Apple / Google — process payments and send us proof of purchase
  • Google (Gmail) — receives emails you send us

These providers use the data only to provide their service to us and must protect it at least as well as this policy requires. Data may be stored on servers outside Thailand.

Other players only see your in-game name and tag number, picture, frame and flag (when you buy from their market stall).

3. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account
  • Old profile pictures: deleted as soon as you change or remove them
  • Reports and anti-cheat alerts: deleted once reviewed, or when an account involved is deleted
  • Moderation, suspension and correction history: kept until the account is deleted · suspended accounts can still be deleted
  • Purchase records (transaction ID with Player ID, no name): kept after account deletion for refunds, restoring purchases, fraud prevention and accounting
  • Emails: no longer than needed to handle your request
  • Deleted data may remain in our providers' backups for a short time until their regular cycle removes it

4. Deleting your account and data

In the Game: Settings → Info & Account → Delete account. If you no longer have the Game, see Delete your account. The account is permanently deleted after 7 days (open the Game before then to cancel).

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction, a copy, or deletion of your data, or object to its use, under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and the laws of your country (e.g. GDPR), by emailing tember.game.studio@gmail.com with your Player ID (Settings → Info & Account). You may also complain to your data protection authority.

6. Children

The Game is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child gave us personal data (such as a photo or real name), email us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the Game if the change is significant.
